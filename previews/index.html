Claude Code Mods

Each game preview is an animation rendered by the mod's own simulation. The work mods show captures from real sessions.

change-journal

A live HTML page that shows every change Claude makes, with diffs, the reason for each edit, checks, and before/after behavior flows.

decision-lens

See the decisions Claude made in each turn, why it made them, and steer future turns with keep and avoid rules.

grill

While Claude works on your task, a sharp partner asks the questions that change the result. Answers reach Claude mid-turn. Brainstorm and side chat modes too.

unslop

Finds AI slop in Claude's changes (dashes, comment blocks, comments that repeat the code, tests that assert nothing, filler phrases) and tells Claude to remove it. Shows what was unslopped.

scope-guard

Notices when Claude drifts outside your request. Clear drift waits for your answer in a question dialog; milder drift is flagged above the prompt with a pull-back key.

looks

Make Claude Code look the way you like: one-line tool calls, replies centered at a reading width, and four themes (Retro CRT, Punk, Synthwave, Zen paper) switched with /theme.

samurai-dojo

A Claude samurai cuts down a Codex, Gemini, or ChatGPT foe for every tool call, and meditates, trains, and duels between them through four seasons.

slayer-corps

Tanjiro, Nezuko, Zenitsu, and Inosuke take turns against demons up to Muzan as you and Claude work, and spar between strikes. The story continues every session.

night-feast

A vampire feeds on every tool call Claude finishes while the moon tracks how full the context window is. Between meals it stalks, swoops, and lies in wait. Dawn means it is time to compact.

little-harvest

Every file that changes becomes a crop: Claude's edits and yours grow a farm in its own pane, tests bring sun or storms, and passing turns fill the barn.

pocket-familiar

A fox spirit that lives on your work: passing tests feed it, finished turns cheer it, it plays while you are quiet, and it grows up to nine tails.
